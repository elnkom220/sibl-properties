How to Verify a Property Before You Pay

A practical guide for property buyers in Nigeria.

Why Property Verification Matters

Buying property is a major financial decision. Before making any payment, a buyer should take reasonable steps to confirm what they are buying, who is offering it, and whether the property and transaction can be properly verified.

1. Identify the Property Clearly

Start by establishing exactly what is being offered. Confirm the property name, location, type of property, plot or unit size, and any other details that identify the particular property.

2. Know Who You Are Dealing With

Ask for the identity and role of the person or organisation selling or marketing the property. If an agent or sales representative is involved, understand who they represent and how their relationship with the property owner or developer can be verified.

3. Ask About the Property Documents

Request information about the documents attached to the property and understand what each document is intended to establish. Depending on the property and location, relevant documentation may include title documents, survey plans, approved building documents and other records.

4. Verify the Information Independently

Do not rely only on photographs, social media posts, brochures or verbal assurances. Where appropriate, have the relevant property information and documents checked through the proper authorities or qualified professionals.

5. Confirm the Payment Details

Before making payment, confirm the exact price, payment plan, applicable fees, payment instructions and the name of the authorised recipient. Make sure the information matches the official transaction documents or instructions.

6. Visit or Inspect the Property

Where possible, inspect the property or development location. A physical inspection can help you understand the surroundings, access, development status and other practical factors that may not be obvious from photographs.

7. Get Professional Advice When Necessary

For significant property transactions, consider engaging qualified professionals, such as a property lawyer, surveyor or other appropriate expert, to independently review matters within their area of expertise.

Simple Buyer Checklist

  • Confirm the exact property and location.
  • Confirm who owns or is authorised to sell the property.
  • Ask for relevant property documents.
  • Verify important information independently.
  • Understand the complete price and payment terms.
  • Inspect the property where possible.
  • Seek professional advice when necessary.
  • Keep copies of important documents and payment records.

Final Thought

A property opportunity should not be evaluated by price alone. Take time to understand the property, the documentation, the transaction and the people involved before committing your money.

Important: This guide is for general educational purposes and does not replace professional legal, surveying, valuation or other specialised advice. Property requirements and verification procedures can vary by location and transaction.
