What to Check Before Buying Land

A practical guide for people considering a land purchase in Nigeria.

Why Careful Checking Matters

Buying land is a major financial commitment. Before paying, take time to understand the land, its location, ownership, documentation, development potential and the terms of the transaction.

1. Confirm the Exact Location

Make sure you know exactly where the land is located. Confirm the estate or area, access roads, surrounding developments and the physical location of the specific plot being offered.

2. Confirm the Land Size

Ask for the stated plot size and compare it with the relevant survey information. Do not assume that descriptions such as "standard plot" mean the same dimensions in every development or location.

3. Understand the Ownership or Interest

Ask who owns the land or holds the interest being offered for sale. Understand whether you are buying directly from an owner, developer or through another authorised party.

4. Check the Relevant Documents

Ask what documents are available for the land and what they establish. Depending on the transaction, these may include title documents, survey information, agreements and relevant approvals.

5. Verify the Land

Documents and claims should be independently verified where appropriate. For a significant transaction, professional assistance can help identify issues that may not be obvious from a brochure or inspection alone.

6. Check Access and Surroundings

Consider road access, drainage, electricity, water, nearby development and other factors that may affect how the land can be used and its future appeal.

7. Understand the Total Cost

The advertised land price may not always represent the complete cost of acquiring or developing the property. Ask about applicable fees, documentation costs, development charges and other transaction expenses.

8. Understand the Payment Terms

If an instalment plan is available, understand the amount required upfront, payment schedule, duration, consequences of late payment and any other conditions before committing.

9. Get the Agreement in Writing

Important terms should be documented clearly. Keep copies of agreements, receipts, payment records and other relevant documents throughout the transaction.

Simple Land-Buying Checklist

  • Confirm the exact location.
  • Confirm the plot size.
  • Understand who owns or controls the land.
  • Ask for and verify relevant documents.
  • Inspect the location where possible.
  • Understand the full cost of the transaction.
  • Review the payment plan and conditions.
  • Keep written records of the transaction.
  • Seek professional advice when necessary.

Final Thought

A good land purchase involves more than finding an attractive price. Take time to understand what you are buying and verify the important information before making a financial commitment.

Important: This guide is for general educational purposes and does not replace professional legal, surveying, valuation or other specialised advice. Requirements can vary depending on the location and circumstances of the transaction.
