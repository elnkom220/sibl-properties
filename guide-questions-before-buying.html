Questions to Ask Before Buying Property

Important questions that can help you make a more informed property decision.

Why Asking the Right Questions Matters

Buying property is a major financial decision. Whether you are buying land, an apartment, a house or another type of property, asking the right questions can help you understand exactly what you are purchasing before you commit your money.

Do not be afraid to ask questions. A serious buyer should understand the property, the seller or developer, the documents, the payment terms and the conditions attached to the transaction.

1. Who Owns or Controls the Property?

Ask who owns the property and who has the authority to sell or transfer the interest in the property.

If you are dealing with a developer, company or representative, understand their role in the transaction and who the contracting party will be.

2. What Documents Are Available?

Ask what documentation exists for the property and what documents you will receive as part of the transaction.

Depending on the property and transaction, this may include documents relating to title, survey, allocation, assignment, building approvals, receipts and other relevant records.

3. Has the Property Been Properly Verified?

Ask how the property's ownership, title and status have been verified.

Do not rely only on verbal assurances. Where necessary, obtain independent professional advice and carry out appropriate searches or verification.

4. What Exactly Am I Buying?

Make sure you understand the exact property being offered to you.

Ask about the location, plot size, property type, unit type, boundaries, specifications and anything else that defines what you are purchasing.

5. What Is the Total Cost?

Do not look only at the advertised purchase price.

Ask whether there are additional charges such as documentation fees, development charges, service charges, legal fees, infrastructure fees, registration costs or other applicable expenses.

6. What Are the Payment Terms?

If the property is being offered through a payment plan, ask about the initial payment, instalments, duration, payment dates and consequences of late payment.

Make sure the payment plan is clearly documented before you begin making payments.

7. What Happens After I Complete Payment?

Ask what happens after you have completed the agreed payment.

Understand the documentation, allocation, possession, handover or transfer process that applies to the particular property.

8. What Is the Development Timeline?

If the property is part of an estate or development, ask about the current development status and any stated timelines for infrastructure, construction or handover.

Where timelines are provided, make sure you understand whether they are estimates, contractual commitments or subject to specific conditions.

9. Can I Inspect the Property?

Ask whether you can physically inspect the property or development site before making a commitment.

An inspection can help you understand the actual location, access, surroundings, development and general condition of the property.

10. What Are the Terms of the Agreement?

Before signing, read the agreement carefully and make sure you understand your responsibilities, the seller's or developer's responsibilities, payment conditions, cancellation provisions and other important terms.

If anything is unclear, ask questions and consider obtaining independent legal advice.

11. Who Can I Contact After Purchase?

Ask who will handle enquiries, documentation, allocation, construction updates, customer service or other matters after your purchase.

Having a clear point of contact can make the process easier after the transaction.

Quick Buyer Checklist

  • Who owns or controls the property?
  • What documents are available?
  • Has the property been independently verified?
  • What exactly am I buying?
  • What is the total cost?
  • Are there additional charges?
  • What are the payment terms?
  • What happens after full payment?
  • Can I inspect the property?
  • What exactly does the agreement say?
  • Who will assist me after purchase?

Final Thought

A good property decision starts with good questions. Take your time, ask for clarification, review the available information and seek appropriate professional advice where necessary.

You should understand what you are buying and the terms of the transaction before committing your money.
